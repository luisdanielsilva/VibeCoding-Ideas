StockSentinel is a web-based stock tracker and alert system for self-directed investors. You build a list of tickers — marking each as Watching (candidate) or Holding (position you own, with cost basis and quantity) — and the app monitors them continuously in real time. When a configured alert condition fires (e.g. a 20% intraday drop), you receive an email within seconds, not at the end of the day. Charts cover the full historical range of a stock with timeframe selectors from 1D to All, and a dedicated Historical Analysis view lets you explore the lowest and highest points a stock ever reached, with annotations marking when they occurred. The platform supports a small number of trusted users: each account has its own watchlist, portfolio, alert rules, and notification settings, all stored in a relational database. An on-demand News panel fetches the latest headlines from the past 3 months (via Google News RSS) for any ticker, shown only when the user explicitly requests it. All values display in EUR or USD.
