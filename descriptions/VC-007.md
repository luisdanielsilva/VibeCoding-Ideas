Every photo carries hidden data: GPS coordinates, device fingerprints, author names, and timestamps embedded invisibly in the file. MetaStrip surfaces all of it and lets you remove exactly what you choose. Upload JPEG, PNG, or TIFF images, inspect metadata by category, pick a preset (Privacy Mode, Full Clean, or Custom), and download stripped images — individually or as a ZIP. Runs as a native Electron desktop app on macOS, Windows, and Linux, or as a self-hosted Express web server for teams.
